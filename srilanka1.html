Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a beautiful island country located in the Indian Ocean, south of India. It is known for its stunning beaches, wildlife, tea plantations, and rich cultural heritage.

History

Sri Lanka has a history that dates back more than 2,500 years. It was home to ancient kingdoms and has been influenced by different cultures and civilizations over time.

Culture

Sri Lankan culture is a mix of traditions, religions, festivals, music, and dance. Buddhism plays an important role in the country’s culture and history.

Tourism

Tourism is growing rapidly in Sri Lanka. Visitors enjoy beaches, historical sites, wildlife safaris, and scenic landscapes.

Famous Places

Popular places in Sri Lanka include Colombo, Kandy, Sigiriya Rock Fortress, Ella, and Yala National Park.
